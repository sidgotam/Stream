India vs New Zealand, 1st T20I — Live Streaming

Hagley Oval, Christchurch • Today 12:30 PM IST • T20I

Match Preview

India start their New Zealand tour with the opening T20I at Hagley Oval, a venue that has always helped the seamers early on. Shreyas Iyer leads a young side, and the top order will be tested against the moving ball in Christchurch conditions.

New Zealand are without Rachin Ravindra, Ben Sears and Will O'Rourke, but Adam Milne returns to add pace. Mitchell Santner will rely on his experienced attack to exploit the early bounce.

Toss will matter — chasing under lights with the ball coming on nicely has been the trend here.
